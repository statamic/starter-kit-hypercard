  <p>Some days call for a few sentences. Others need <strong>a little more room</strong>. This is a page for all the things that might find their way into a journal: photographs, recipes, lists, quotations, and the occasional very important table.</p>
  <p>You can write <em>quietly</em>, add <strong><em>a little emphasis</em></strong>, cross out <del>an unwise decision</del>, or <mark>remember something for later</mark>. A link to <a href="https://statamic.com">somewhere good</a> should look like a link.</p>

  <h2>A place for everything</h2>
  <p>A heading gives a long entry a little breathing room. The words still do most of the work.</p>
  <h3>The smaller details</h3>
  <p>Subheadings belong to the same family, just a little quieter. Here’s an abbreviation: <abbr title="Personal digital assistant">PDA</abbr>. And a small aside<sup><a href="#note-1" id="reference-1" aria-label="Read footnote 1">1</a></sup>.</p>
  <h4>A note in the margin</h4>
  <p>There’s room for smaller headings, too.</p>
  <h5>One more detail</h5>
  <p>Small, but still clearly a heading.</p>
  <h6>The fine print</h6>
  <p>Even the smallest heading gets its own place.</p>

  We drove until the buildings thinned out and the road started following the river. No particular destination, which is my favorite kind.

  There was a place to pull over. We ate sandwiches on a stone wall and watched a dog repeatedly retrieve the same stick. He seemed to have things figured out.

  On the way back, we missed the turn. Neither of us mentioned it for a while.

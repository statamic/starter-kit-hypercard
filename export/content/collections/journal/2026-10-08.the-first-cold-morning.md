content_format: markdown

markdown_content: |-
  The floor was cold enough to send me looking for the slippers I haven’t seen since April. They were under the bed, exactly where you’d expect.

  Outside, everyone had made a different decision about the weather. One man in shorts. One woman in a scarf big enough for two. I wore the wrong coat.

  The coffee shop had turned the heating on. I stayed for a second cup.

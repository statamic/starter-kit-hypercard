title: 'The long way home'
content_format: markdown
show_illustration: true
markdown_content: |-
  I took the long way home today. Past the old cinema, over the bridge, and through the streets where nothing much has changed.

  There’s a house on the corner with a pear tree that leans over the fence. Someone had left a box beneath it. “Please take a few,” the sign said. So I did.

  I got home twenty minutes late with three pears in my coat pockets and nothing much to report. Some days are worth keeping, even when nothing happens.

content_format: markdown

markdown_content: |-
  A train ticket from 1988. A shopping list: milk, matches, something I couldn’t read. One very flat leaf.

  The book cost two dollars. The woman at the shop asked if I wanted a bag, then told me it was her favorite. She couldn’t remember how it ended.

  I’m leaving everything where I found it. It seems wrong to separate a book from the things it’s been carrying around.

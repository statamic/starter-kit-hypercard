  Bought a book for a nickel. Found a pressed flower, half a train ticket, and a map tucked inside. Man at the shop said the map led to a fortune. Asked why he was still selling books.

  Followed it anyway. Found a hollow tree with six cents in it. A cent up, not counting the afternoon.

  Hosea asked whether I'd learned anything. Told him the book wasn't bad.

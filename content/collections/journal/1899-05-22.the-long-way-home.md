  Sucked rattlesnake venom out of another stranger today. Third time this week. Beginning to think they are doing it on purpose.

  He asked what he owed me. Told him to buy some boots. Preferably ones that go all the way up to wherever he keeps putting the snake.

  Got back to camp after dark. Dutch asked why I hadn't brought any money. Explained about the man and the snake. He said we all have our burdens. Went back to his tent.
